# Meritocratic Code of Conduct

## 1. Core Philosophy
Wanda is developed with a strict focus on software excellence, battery optimization, architectural discipline, and user privacy. Participation in this project is governed purely by technical merit and productive collaboration.

We do not evaluate contributors on personal beliefs, demographic classifications, social background, or external opinions. Work in this project stands or falls solely on its engineering rationale, execution quality, and tangible value to the software.

---

## 2. Standards of Collaboration

### Technical Merit as the Sole Metric
* Code reviews, architectural decisions, and feature additions are evaluated on correctness, maintainability, performance, security, and conformity with project guidelines.
* Debates must remain strictly technical. Arguments from authority, emotional appeals, and ideological posturing carry zero weight; empirical evidence, benchmarks, tests, and sound engineering logic do.

### Critique the Patch, Not the Person
* Code review can be direct, rigorous, and unvarnished. Robust technical critique is an essential component of building high-quality systems and should be received as an objective evaluation of the code, not a personal attack.
* Contributors are expected to substantiate engineering choices calmly and accept technical feedback gracefully. Conversely, critique must be directed exclusively at the code, architecture, or documentation—never at the character or personal traits of the author.

### Scope and Neutrality
* All communication within project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews) must relate directly to the technical development and maintenance of Wanda.
* External political discussions, social activism, cultural debates, and off-topic soapboxing are strictly out of scope. The repository tracker is not a public forum for non-technical disputes.

### Professional Conduct
* Maintain professional civility. Flaming, trolling, harassment, bad-faith obstruction, spamming, and personal insults degrade technical collaboration and will not be tolerated.

---

## 3. Enforcement and Project Governance

The maintainers of Wanda hold ultimate discretion over repository governance to protect project velocity, software quality, and focused collaboration.

* **Thread Moderation:** Discussions that veer off-topic into personal hostility, ideological debates, or non-technical arguments will be closed, locked, or hidden.
* **Contribution Rejection:** Contributions that fail to meet technical constraints, introduce unnecessary overhead, or bypass architectural requirements will be rejected on merit.
* **Removal of Disruptive Actors:** Individuals who repeatedly instigate off-topic conflicts, engage in bad-faith disruptions, or refuse to maintain technical professionalism will have their access to project spaces temporarily or permanently revoked.

Concerns regarding disruption, bad-faith conduct, or violations within project spaces may be reported directly to the maintainers at `contact@kolbxyz.xyz`.
